FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is video generation really free? +
Yes. Unlike credit-based tools, NexGen runs the pipeline on your PC with no per-video fees, no credits, and nothing expiring. Make 10 videos or 1,000 — your cost per video is $0.
What does "consistent characters" mean? +
Define a character once with reference images and a name. NexGen keeps the same face, outfit and look across every scene in the video — and across videos in a series. Most AI video tools regenerate a slightly different person every shot.
What niches does it work for? +
Any. True crime, finance, history, horror, geography, kids' stories, motivation, documentaries, anime recaps — pick the visual style that fits the niche, or build a custom one. You're not locked to one look.
How long does a video take? +
About 30–45 minutes for a full-length video, regardless of length — the pipeline parallelizes voiceover, visuals and editing. Queue multiple videos in batch mode and wake up to finished MP4s.
Windows only? +
Yes, v3 is a Windows desktop app. That's what lets it run locally with zero per-video cost.
Can I use it for client work? +
The Agency tier includes commercial client-work rights. Solo and Creator cover your own channels.
What if I need help? +
Community support for Solo, priority support for Creator, and a 1-on-1 onboarding call with Agency. Plus video tutorials for every major workflow.
